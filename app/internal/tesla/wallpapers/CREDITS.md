# Chat wallpaper credits

The built-in chat wallpapers are freely licensed photos chosen to resemble the
subjects of Google Messages' wallpaper categories. None are Google's images.
They come from Wikimedia Commons and were resized (and, in Black & White,
converted to grayscale) for this app. Every file is public domain, CC0, or
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY), which requires the
attribution below. No share-alike (CC BY-SA) or non-commercial images are used.

License summary: CC BY 2.0: 26, CC BY 2.5: 2, CC BY 3.0: 11, CC BY 4.0: 17, CC0: 13, Public domain: 21
